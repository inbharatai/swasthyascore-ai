Height Measurement Marker

Use this marker for accurate AI-assisted height measurement in the SwasthyaScore AI camera screen.

⚠️ PRINT AT EXACTLY 100% SCALE — DO NOT "SCALE TO FIT" OR "FIT TO PAGE"
This marker must be exactly 180 mm × 180 mm after printing.
  1. Print this page at 100% scale — check your printer settings and disable "Fit to Page" / "Scale to Fit".
  2. After printing, verify the marker is exactly 180 mm × 180 mm using a ruler. If it is not, reprint at the correct scale.
  3. Tape or hold the marker flat on a wall at approximately chest height of the person being measured.
  4. Open the SwasthyaScore AI app, go to Camera Assist → Height → Marker Mode.
  5. Stand 1–2 meters from the camera. Keep your full body visible with head and feet in frame.
  6. Wait for "Marker detected ✓" on screen before tapping Capture.
  7. The app will show an estimated height and confidence level. High confidence results can be applied directly.
